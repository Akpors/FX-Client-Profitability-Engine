Project 3 — FX Client Profitability Engine
Terrain: Financial Services / FX

This one should become one of your signature projects.

Create a realistic synthetic FX transaction dataset:

Client → Trade → Currency Pair → Buy Rate → Sell Rate → Benchmark Rate → Volume → Timestamp → P&L

Then calculate:

- trading volume
- gross spread
- benchmark spread
- client profitability
- profitability per currency
- profitability per trade
- average ticket size
- client concentration
- revenue contribution
- execution quality
- spread compression
- client profitability trends
Use ECB exchange-rate data as an independent benchmark source. The ECB provides programmatic access through its SDMX API, including daily exchange-rate series. European Central Bank Data

The interesting analytical question is:
“Which clients look valuable based on volume but are actually low-margin?”

Now you've moved from BI to commercial decision intelligence.

Extra depth: create a pricing recommendation:
Client A generates ₦X in volume but only ₦Y in economic contribution. Should pricing change?

That's a much stronger portfolio story.
